LearnCad
=================

LearnCad is a guided CAD workspace for learning by building. It watches the
model, points out the next step, and opens the right tool without getting in
the way.

Before installing:

1. Close LearnCad.
2. Make sure the LearnCad modeling engine has been installed and started once.

What this installer does:

- Installs LearnCad into your user profile.
- Adds the LearnCad launcher, icon, and Start Menu shortcut.
- Creates an uninstall entry in Windows Apps & Features.
- Creates a desktop shortcut named LearnCad.
- Leaves your models and your settings alone.

After installing:

1. Open LearnCad from the desktop shortcut or the Start Menu.
2. Type what you are making, then follow the coach.

Uninstalling removes the application files. Your settings remain in your user
profile. Remove that folder manually if you want a completely clean uninstall.
