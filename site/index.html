Questions

Do I need to know CAD first?

No. Start with a real part and follow the coach. The goal is to internalize the review so you need it less over time.

Is LearnCad free?

Yes. The local coach is free, and the community course catalog is free. AI explanations are optional and use your own provider or a LearnCad cloud subscription.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The deterministic coach runs locally with no network. Only an AI explanation request uses the network, and only after you enable it.

Can I publish a course?

Yes. Courses are data-only .learncad packages submitted by pull request. See the course guide.

Does LearnCad lock my models in?

No. LearnCad works on standard CAD files and the feature tree. You keep the model and can export STL or STEP.
